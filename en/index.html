Innovative Solutions in Precision Grinding and Machining

Advancing Precision Manufacturing with CHEVALIER
#

CHEVALIER, the brand of FALCON Machine Tools Co., Ltd., stands at the forefront of grinding, milling, and turning technology. Our commitment is to shape ideas into reality by listening to customer needs and translating them into practical, innovative solutions. With a global presence, we continuously strive for excellence in technological advancement and sustainable practices.

Our Vision and Values
#

We are dedicated to providing comprehensive solutions that address the evolving requirements of modern industries. Our vision is rooted in continuous improvement, technological innovation, and a strong focus on green transformation. By fostering a culture of collaboration and expertise, we ensure that every product and service aligns with the highest standards of quality and reliability.

Company Milestones
#

With decades of experience, CHEVALIER has built a reputation for delivering high-precision machinery and tailored solutions. Our journey is marked by significant milestones in research and development, equipment upgrades, and the expansion of our assembly lines. These achievements reflect our unwavering commitment to meeting the needs of a dynamic market.

Core Competencies
#

  • Research & Development: Our R&D team drives innovation, ensuring that our products remain at the cutting edge of technology.
  • Advanced Equipment: We invest in state-of-the-art machinery to deliver superior performance and precision.
  • Efficient Assembly Lines: Streamlined processes enable us to maintain high production standards and timely delivery.
  • Quality Control: Rigorous quality assurance measures guarantee the reliability and durability of our products.

ESG Commitment
#

CHEVALIER integrates Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles into our operations. Our environmental policy emphasizes sustainable practices, while our human rights and CSR policies ensure ethical conduct and stakeholder engagement throughout our value chain.

SMART+ Intelligent Machinery
#

Our proprietary SMART iControl and iSurface CNC systems are designed to enhance the performance of grinding machines, simplifying operations and improving efficiency. These intelligent solutions empower manufacturers to achieve greater precision and productivity.

Industry Applications
#

CHEVALIER’s solutions cater to a wide range of industries, each with unique demands for precision and innovation:

  • Aerospace Industry: Our team delivers machining solutions for advanced materials and special alloys, ensuring components meet stringent precision standards. Learn more
  • Semiconductor Industry: We provide equipment capable of processing hard and brittle materials, supporting high-speed, automated, and precise manufacturing. Learn more
  • Automotive Industry: Our expertise extends to electric vehicle components, such as slot dies and ultrasonic welding heads, focusing on mirror surface grinding and dimensional accuracy. Learn more
